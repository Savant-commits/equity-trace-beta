Getting Started
5 topics
▶
How do I add my first trade?
▶
Click the + Add Trade button in the top-right corner, or press N on your keyboard. Fill in the required fields — symbol, direction, entry/exit prices, date, time, and P&L. Optional fields like setup, session, and notes help build better analytics over time.
How do I add an account?
▶
Go to Settings → Accounts and click + Add Account. Enter your prop firm name, phase, account size, and drawdown limits. Each account is tracked independently and can be selected from the account switcher in the header.
Where is my data stored?
▶
All your data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server. This means your data stays on your device, but clearing browser data or uninstalling the app will erase it.
How do I back up or transfer my data?
▶
The app stores trades locally, and the current settings experience no longer exposes import/export controls in the Data section. If you need to keep your journal safe, avoid clearing browser storage and use the available report export tools elsewhere in the app for summaries and backups.
What does the version number mean?
▶
The app is currently in a preview release with the journal, accounts, and analytics features available. It is stable for daily tracking but still being refined, so report any issues and your current workflow if you encounter one.
Dashboard & Overview
5 topics
▶
What does "All Accounts" mode show?
▶
When All Accounts is selected, the dashboard aggregates data across all your accounts — total P&L, combined win rate, and portfolio overview. The Daily P&L chart switches to dollar values instead of percentages since each account has a different size.
Why does the Profit Factor show infinity?
▶
Profit Factor = Total Wins ÷ Total Losses. If you have no losing trades (or haven't entered any losses yet), the denominator is zero — which results in ∞. This is mathematically correct and means your trading has been 100% profitable so far.
What is the Setup Scorecard?
▶
The Setup Scorecard ranks your trades by a composite score based on win/loss result, risk management quality, kill zone alignment, instrument traded, and win streak multiplier.
What are the Goals cards tracking?
▶
The Goals section shows Max Loss, Profit Target, and Daily Loss metrics configured per account. These are the main prop firm rules to monitor while trading.
How does the Equity Curve work?
▶
The equity curve plots your running account balance after each trade. Use filters to zoom into a specific period and click any point to view that trade’s detail.
Trade Journal
5 topics
▶
How do I edit a trade?
▶
Click any trade row in the Trade Journal to open the detail panel, then click the Edit button to update the trade.
How do I add screenshots to a trade?
▶
Use drag & drop, paste from clipboard, or file browse in the trade detail screenshot section.
What is the Rating (stars) for?
▶
The rating captures execution quality separate from profitability. Use it to compare trade quality over time.
How do I filter trades?
▶
Use the filter bar at the top of the Trade Journal to choose direction, result, symbol, session, date, and account filters.
What is Bulk Edit?
▶
Select multiple trades using the checkboxes and then use the action bar to update account, session, setup, or model fields in bulk.
Analytics & Charts
4 topics
▶
What is the Heatmap showing?
▶
The calendar heatmap shows daily P&L at a glance. Green days are profitable and red days are losses.
What does the Daily P&L chart show?
▶
It plots net P&L for each day, with green rightward bars for profit and red leftward bars for loss.
What is the P&L by Session chart?
▶
It breaks down P&L by London, New York, Asia, and other trading sessions so you can compare performance by time zone.
How is R-Multiple calculated?
▶
R-Multiple = Exit P&L ÷ Risk Amount. For example, risking $100 and making $150 results in 1.5R.

What are the three themes?
▶
  • Midnight - Pure black background for low-light focus.
  • Aura - Dark grey with vivid accent fills.
  • Dawn - Light background for daytime use.
My theme resets every time I open the app
▶
Theme preferences are saved in localStorage. If your browser clears site data on close or you're in private mode, theme settings may not persist.
Prop Firm & Accounts
4 topics
▶
How do I set up a prop firm account?
▶
In Settings → Accounts, click + Add Account and fill in the firm name, phase, account size, drawdown limits, and profit target.
What is the difference between Trailing and Static drawdown?
▶
  • Static - Drawdown is fixed from your starting balance.
  • Trailing - Drawdown follows your best equity point as you profit.
How does the Daily Loss timer reset work?
▶
The timer counts down to the next daily reset, typically midnight local time or the firm's reset time, and then resets your daily P&L counter.
Can I track multiple accounts at once?
▶
Yes. Add multiple accounts and choose All Accounts to view combined metrics, or select a single account to view its specific performance.
Troubleshooting
4 topics
▶
My data disappeared after opening the app
▶
This can happen if the browser cleared localStorage, or if you're in incognito/private mode. Avoid clearing site data and keep backups if possible.
Charts are not displaying correctly
▶
Refresh the page, disable extensions that block scripts, or use a modern browser like Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
The app looks different on my phone
▶
Equity Trace is optimized for desktop. On smaller screens, some panels condense and additional scrolling may be required.
A keyboard shortcut is not working
▶
Shortcuts are disabled when an input field is focused or a modal is open. Click outside the form first and try again.
